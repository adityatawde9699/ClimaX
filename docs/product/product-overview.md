# ClimaX Product Overview

## 1. Product Vision & Mission

**ClimaX** is a next-generation **Federated AI Environmental Intelligence & Action Platform** that transforms fragmented environmental data into decisive municipal intervention and citizen empowerment.

Traditional air quality solutions function as passive informational displays: they report that the air is hazardous after citizens have already inhaled toxic particulate. ClimaX transitions environmental governance from **passive observation** to **predictive, accountable intervention**.

---

## 2. The Closed-Loop Operational Cycle

```
Detect ──► Understand ──► Predict ──► Prioritize ──► Act ──► Measure ──► (Detect)
```

1. **Detect**: Combines crowd-sourced citizen visual reports, IoT sensor meshes, satellite rasters, and government monitoring stations into a unified spatial canvas.
2. **Understand**: Evaluates unstructured media and sensor telemetry using Gemini 1.5 Multimodal AI to identify emission types, opacity, and visual plumes with explainable justifications.
3. **Predict**: Forecasts localized 6h-72h plume trajectories and AQI spikes using physics-informed ML models hosted on Vertex AI.
4. **Prioritize**: Dynamically scores environmental risk by intersecting pollutant concentrations with population vulnerability (schools, hospitals, elder care facilities).
5. **Act**: Converts critical hazards into verified municipal incidents, dispatching anti-smog guns, road sprinklers, and field enforcement officers.
6. **Measure**: Continuously tracks post-intervention sensor readings to quantify atmospheric improvement, calculating the true causal return on municipal intervention investments.
