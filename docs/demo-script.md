# ClimaX three-minute demo

1. Open `/map`, show live sensors and the AQI heat layer.
2. Submit a citizen report, then show its AI analysis and inferred evidence tier.
3. Open an active incident, assign an officer, and dispatch an intervention.
4. Show the prediction horizon selector and risk hotspot view.
5. Open the authority copilot and ask for the worst AQI area.
6. Complete the intervention and show the PM2.5 effectiveness measurement.

Run `python scripts/seed-db.py --env development` then `python scripts/seed-demo.py` after migrations.
